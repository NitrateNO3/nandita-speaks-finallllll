# Editing guide for Nandita's team

Sign in at **nanditaspeaks.in/admin** with your email and password. After five wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes.

## Read and manage enquiries
1. Open **Enquiries**. The newest is at the top.
2. Click an enquiry to read it. Change **Status** (New, Contacted, In discussion, Confirmed, Closed) in the right-hand column and press **Save**.
3. Use **Internal notes** for anything the team needs to remember. Visitors never see it.
4. To download a spreadsheet, use the **Export CSV** buttons above the list (all, or one status).
5. Every enquiry is saved here first, even if an email fails. "Delivery and spam checks" shows whether the emails were sent.

## Change page text
1. Open **Pages** and choose Home, About, Speaking, Contact or Blog index.
2. Every field starts with the approved Page Copy. Edit and press **Save**. The website updates within a minute.
3. Write `*Silent Screams*` for italics and `[my About page](/about/)` for a link.
4. Leave a field empty to keep that line hidden (for example "Story extra line" or "Anthem").
5. Two switches on the Speaking page control approval: **Talks approved** and **Bio approved**. Until ticked, visitors do not see those sections.

## Photos
1. Upload under **Content › Media**. Describe what the photo shows in **Alt** (for example "Nandita Thakur singing").
2. Choose where each photo appears under **Pages › Photographs**. An empty slot is simply not shown.
3. Use only real photographs of Nandita.

## The book
Under **Pages › Silent Screams**: cover, description, synopsis and purchase link. The **Buy the Book** button appears only after **Purchase confirmed** is ticked.

## Contact details and social links
Under **Settings › Contact and social**. Each item has a "Show" switch. Add where enquiry emails should go in **Notify emails** and the promised **Reply time**.

## Write a blog post
1. **Blog › Posts › Create new.** Add title, category, featured image (with alt text), excerpt and the post.
2. Press **Preview** to see the draft on the site (only signed-in editors can see it).
3. To schedule, set **Published at** to a future date and time, then press **Publish**. It appears automatically at that time.
4. Open **Search and social sharing** to set a custom title, description or share image.
5. Manage categories under **Blog › Categories**.

The Home page shows "From the blog" once three posts are published.
