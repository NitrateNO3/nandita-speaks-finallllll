Who I speak to

For women’s communities and events

I speak at International Women’s Day sessions, women’s network meetings, association and community gatherings, and conferences for women. confirm event types with Nandita

Some of the women in the room will have been through a major setback. Others will have spent years meeting everyone else’s expectations and slowly lost confidence in themselves. My life will not match every woman’s, and I do not tell anyone what to decide. I share what happened to me and ask questions.

For companies and teams

I also speak to teams and middle managers about rebuilding self-belief after a setback, making your own decisions and keeping a life of your own outside work. The audience can be women only or a mixed group. These are talks that start a conversation. I do not run a leadership programme.

What I speak about

01

Believing in yourself again

Confidence can drop after a major setback, or fade slowly over years of putting other people first. I talk about how that happens and what a first step back can look like.

02

Having an identity of your own

Family and work can take over every hour of the day. I talk about keeping a few interests that belong only to you, and why they matter.

03

Building independence

Earning my own money gave me the freedom to make my own decisions. I share what that was like for me. It is my own experience and not financial advice.

04

Living through uncertainty

I looked after both my parents through cancer and then had cancer myself. I talk about what it is like when plans change and what I did next.

05

Finding enjoyment again

I kept singing through chemotherapy. I talk about making room for music, laughter and ordinary pleasures even when life is hard.

What Do I Do With My Life Now?

Many women reach a point where they are no longer sure what they want or what they can do. It can come after a major setback or after years of meeting everyone else’s expectations. In this talk I share how I had to rebuild parts of my own life more than once, and what I did next.

Questions the audience takes home

  • What has happened to my belief in myself?
  • What is one decision I can begin making for myself?
  • What is a next step that feels manageable?

Silent Screams: The Courage to Reclaim Your Life

This talk draws on the themes of my book, Silent Screams: independence, having an identity of your own and making your own decisions. I talk about earning my own money, what that changed for me and why I value a life of my own alongside family responsibilities.

Questions the audience takes home

  • What would help me feel more independent?
  • Which interest have I stopped making time for?

Still Singing: Keeping Something of Your Own When Life Gets Hard

I carried on with my music classes and kept singing through chemotherapy. This talk uses that experience to talk about living through uncertainty, keeping a little room for what you enjoy and finding something to laugh about when things go wrong.

Questions the audience takes home

  • What is only mine, and how could I make room for it in a hard stretch?
  • People cope in different ways, so what works for me?
