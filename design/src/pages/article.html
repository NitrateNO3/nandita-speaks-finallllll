TemplateThe text below describes the rich-text styles. It is not article copy. Real posts come from the client. Each post gets Article structured data with Nandita Thakur as author.

Body paragraphs are Manrope 18 px with 1.8 line height, at a reading width of about 700 px. Paragraph spacing is a little over one line, so long first-person writing stays easy to follow on a phone.

Subheading (H2) inside a post

Subheadings use Marcellus. Posts can also contain H3s, inline links underlined in copper, images with captions and embedded video that loads only when played.

Pull quotes sit between a copper hairline and the reading column, in Marcellus.
  • Bulleted lists use copper markers
  • Numbered lists follow the same spacing
  • Images can run at reading width or wider
